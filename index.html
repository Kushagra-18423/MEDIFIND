MediFind

Find out which nearby medical store has your medicine, before you go there.

Search a medicine to see which stores have it.

What makes MediFind different

  • Search by medicine name, salt or problem.
  • If a medicine is out of stock, we show the same salt available in that store.
  • Compare prices and see which stores are open right now.
  • Users can report stock, so the information stays fresh.

Demo with sample data. Stores, prices and stock shown here are not real.

MediFind only shows availability. It does not give medical advice. Ask a doctor or pharmacist before taking any medicine.
